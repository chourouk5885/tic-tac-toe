How the AI works

Tic-Tac-Toe is small enough for the computer to imagine every way the rest of the game could go. It uses an algorithm called minimax.

For each move it could make, it plays the game out in its head, assuming you always answer with your best move. Finished games get a score: a win is good, a loss is bad, a draw is neutral. A quicker win scores a little higher than a slow one.

The scores flow back up: on its own turn the computer takes the highest score, on yours it expects the lowest. Then it plays the move with the best guaranteed result.

Alpha-beta pruning makes this faster. As soon as one line of play is clearly worse than something already found, the computer stops exploring it.

Perfect play from both sides always ends in a draw, so on Unbeatable the best you can do is tie. Turn on Show AI thinking to see what every square leads to.
